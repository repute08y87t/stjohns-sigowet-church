About Us

A.C.K St John Church Sigowet is a vibrant Anglican parish located in Baharini Centre, Turbo Sub-county, Uasin Gishu County.

We are part of the ACK Diocese of Eldoret under the spiritual leadership of The Rt. Rev. Dr. Christopher Ruto.

Our parish head is Kapchumba Parish, serving the Kiplombe Ward community.

Our Vision

To be a transformative church that spreads the Gospel and serves the community through faith, hope, and love.

Our Ministries

KAMA - Kenya Anglican Men's Association
Mothers' Union - Women's fellowship & family ministry
KAYO - Kenya Anglican Youth Organization
Brigades - Boys' & Girls' Brigade
